01 — The Player

Forty years on stage. Still playing.

Hi, I’m Stevie, but most people know me as C-Note. I’ve been a professional musician for over 40 years, playing in rock, blues, jazz, funk, reggae, heavy metal, orchestra and dance party bands, mostly in Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs.

I’ve also been teaching for more than 25 years and have given thousands of lessons on drums, guitar, bass and beginner piano. Today I run the C-Note Music School. Many of my students have gone from complete beginners to professional musicians playing in bands.

— Stevie “C-Note” Cunningham

06 — The Method

Lessons that feel like jamming, never a grind.

01/04
  1. Tailored

    Built around you

    Every lesson is shaped to your abilities and your musical goals. Total beginner or seasoned player, we start where you are and go where you want to go.

  2. Never a grind

    Fun & interactive

    Music should be a joy, not a chore. Lessons are hands-on and engaging, so you leave every session excited to pick up your instrument.

  3. Real band experience

    Play with a band

    I teach with accompaniment and ensemble styles, playing along with you live, so you get real-time experience of what it’s like to be in a band and play with other musicians.

  4. Studio or home

    Everything’s here

    My studio has every instrument you need, plus rehearsal and recording rooms. No gear? No problem, just bring yourself. Prefer to learn at home? In-home lessons are available too.

My goal is to spread the wonderful gift of music to as many people as possible — because music is good for the soul!

— C-Note
